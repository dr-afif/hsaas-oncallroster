User Guide: Accessing with Temporary UPM Email

1 Request Access

If you have a temporary UPM email (e.g., @student.upm.edu.my or @upm.edu.my) and cannot use Google Sign-in, you must first request manual access.

  • Go to the Signup Page.
  • Enter your full name and valid UPM email.
  • Select your department and the duration of access needed (e.g., 6 months).
  • Submit the request and wait for admin verification.

2 Wait for Approval

Administrators will review your request. During this time, if you try to log in, you will see a pending status.

  • Approval usually takes 1-2 working days.
  • Once approved, your email will be authorized for the OTP login system.

3 Receive Login Code

Once approved, you can log in using your UPM email without a password.

  • Go to the Login Page.
  • Enter your approved UPM email in the bottom field.
  • Click "Send 6-Digit Code".
  • Check your UPM mailbox (and junk folder) for a message from Supabase/HSAAS.

4 Verify and Enter

Finalize your login by entering the unique code sent to your email.

  • Enter the 6-digit code in the field provided.
  • Click "Verify" to enter the dashboard.
  • Your session is now active and you can access the roster.
